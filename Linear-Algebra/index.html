How to train with this site

  1. Learn a chapter with your lecture slides and the textbook sections listed on the Sources page.
  2. Quiz it: do the three sets of that chapter. Read every explanation, also for the questions you got right. Use "Redo only the mistakes".
  3. Go deeper in the practice bank: tick written mode to hide the options, solve on paper, then compare with the model answer.
  4. Simulate the exams: the mock midterm (no going back), then the 30-question / 20-minute speed exam. Aim for 40 seconds per question and review every explanation afterwards.
  5. Repeat the weak chapters shown in the progress panel above until each is above 75%.

About the course (Course advice sheet by Nguyen Hai Long (AY 2025–26; lecturer Dao Quang Duc))

ComponentWeightDetails
Attendance10%you are barred from the final if you miss more than 30% of sessions without a valid reason
Midterm30%written test or MCQ on Moodle, depending on the tutorial lecturer; up to 6 bonus points from tutorials can be added to it
Final60%written test, 120 minutes, 5 questions with 2–3 parts each; calculators are not allowed in the midterm and final (the 2023–24 Shift 1 paper allowed them)
  • Main textbook: Elementary Linear Algebra (8th edition) — the course content and many exercises come from it; Strang's book is better for review after you have learned the material.
  • Vector spaces (chapter 4) is the make-or-break topic: eigenvalues, inner products and linear maps all depend on it.
  • Calculations are easy but long and there is no calculator: practise exact fractions, and show every row operation.
