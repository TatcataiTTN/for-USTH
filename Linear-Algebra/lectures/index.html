Unofficial self-study material prepared from the USTH Linear Algebra course documents (not endorsed by USTH). Problem statements are paraphrased with their source cited on every question; solutions, explanations and checks are our own. Every numerical answer is computed by program (sympy / exact rational arithmetic) and, where the course gives an answer key, compared with it; differences are listed on the Sources page. Progress is stored only in your browser.
