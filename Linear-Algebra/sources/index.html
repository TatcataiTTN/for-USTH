Errors and oddities found in the course documents

Each item below was found by checking our computed answer against the printed one (or when an exercise turned out to be impossible as printed). The quiz uses the computed result.

  • Exercise Sheet 3, Ex. 8: The third equation is printed as $5x+4y+2x=4$; read as $5x+4y+2z=4$ (typo).
  • Exercise Sheet 3, Ex. 11: As printed, the matrix $\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 \\ -1 & -1 & -2\end{bmatrix}$ has $\det A=0$, so the inverse requested in the exercise does not exist (probably a typo in the sheet). We keep the exercise as a singular-matrix question: $A\,\operatorname{adj}A=(\det A)I=0$.
  • Exercise Sheet 5, Ex. 16: As printed, $x=(-1,4,2)$ is not in the span of $B=\{(0,2,-2),(1,0,-2)\}$ (the third coordinate would have to be $-2$). We use $x=(-1,4,-2)$, which is in the subspace as the exercise intends.

How answers were verified

  • All answers are produced by code (sympy exact arithmetic, never typed by hand) and, for source problems, compared with the printed answer key; the build stops if they disagree.
  • Distractors are realistic mistakes (sign errors, missing chain-rule factor, transposed matrices, wrong-order composition…), and every distractor is checked to be different from the correct answer.
  • Option positions are shuffled with a fixed seed and balanced (A/B/C/D each 25%); the correct option is not systematically the longest.
University of Science and Technology of Hanoi · B1 · Linear Algebra (MAT1.002)

Unofficial self-study material prepared from the USTH Linear Algebra course documents (not endorsed by USTH). Problem statements are paraphrased with their source cited on every question; solutions, explanations and checks are our own. Every numerical answer is computed by program (sympy / exact rational arithmetic) and, where the course gives an answer key, compared with it; differences are listed on the Sources page. Progress is stored only in your browser.
