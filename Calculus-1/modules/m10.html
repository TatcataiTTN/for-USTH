After this module you can

  • Choose between substitution, parts, trigonometric identities, trigonometric substitution and partial fractions.
  • Evaluate standard integrals fast and verify them by differentiation.
  • Recognise the pattern at a glance: LIATE for parts, identities for trigonometric powers, $\sqrt{a^2\pm x^2}$ and $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$ for trigonometric substitution.

Integration by parts

$$\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$$
  • Choose $u$ by LIATE: Logarithm, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trigonometric, Exponential; $dv$ the rest.
  • $\int x\cos x\,dx=x\sin x+\cos x$; $\int x^2e^xdx=(x^2-2x+2)e^x$.
  • Cyclic: $\int e^x\cos x\,dx=\frac{e^x(\sin x+\cos x)}{2}$ (apply parts twice and solve for the integral).

Trigonometric integrals

IntegralIdea
$\sin^m x\cos^n x$, $n$ oddsave one $\cos x$, $u=\sin x$
$m,n$ evenhalf-angle: $\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}2$
$\tan^m x\sec^n x$, $n$ evensave $\sec^2x$, $u=\tan x$
$\sin ax\cos bx$product-to-sum

Examples: $\int\cos^5x\,dx=\sin x-\frac23\sin^3x+\frac15\sin^5x$; $\int\tan^4x\,dx=\frac{\tan^3x}3-\tan x+x$.

Trigonometric substitution

ContainsSubstitutionIdentity
$\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$$x=a\sin\theta$$1-\sin^2=\cos^2$
$\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$$x=a\tan\theta$$1+\tan^2=\sec^2$
$\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$$x=a\sec\theta$$\sec^2-1=\tan^2$

Draw the right triangle to convert back. $\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4+x^2}}=\ln\left(x+\sqrt{4+x^2}\right)+C$.

Partial fractions

  • Proper fraction $\frac PQ$ ($\deg P<\deg Q$); else divide first: $\frac{2x^3-2x^2+1}{x^2-x}=2x+\frac1{x-1}-\frac1x$.
  • Factor $Q$: linear factors give $\frac A{x-r}$ (repeated: $\frac{A_1}{x-r}+\frac{A_2}{(x-r)^2}+\cdots$); irreducible quadratics give $\frac{Bx+C}{x^2+px+q}$.
  • Cover-up: $\frac{x^2+4x+1}{(x-1)(x+1)(x+3)}$: $A=\frac34$, $B=\frac12$, $C=-\frac14$.
  • Integrate: logs for linear factors, $\arctan$ for quadratics.

Worked examples from the course problems

Worked example 1 · Techniques of integration (Tutorial 9, Q1(b))
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{e^{-\cot z}}{\sin^2z}\,dz$.
Show the answer and the solution

Answer: $e^{- \frac{\cos{\left(z \right)}}{\sin{\left(z \right)}}}+C$

$u=-\cot z$, $du=\csc^2z\,dz$: $\int e^u\,du=e^u$. Check: differentiating gives the integrand. Answer: $e^{- \frac{\cos{\left(z \right)}}{\sin{\left(z \right)}}}+C$.
Worked example 2 · Integration by parts (Tutorial 9, Q4(b))
$\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}x^3\cos2x\,dx=$
Show the answer and the solution

Answer: $\frac{3}{4} - \frac{3 \pi^{2}}{16}$

Integrate by parts three times (tabular method): $x^3\cdot\frac{\sin2x}2+3x^2\frac{\cos2x}{4}-6x\frac{\sin2x}{8}-6\frac{\cos2x}{16}$ evaluated from $0$ to $\frac\pi2$; the value is $\frac{3}{4} - \frac{3 \pi^{2}}{16}\approx-1.1006$.
Worked example 3 · Trigonometric substitution (Tutorial 10, Q1(b))
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \sqrt{1-9t^2}\,dt$.
Show the answer and the solution

Answer: $\frac{t \sqrt{1 - 9 t^{2}}}{2} + \frac{\arcsin{\left(3 t \right)}}{6}+C$

$3t=\sin\theta$: $\frac13\int\cos^2\theta\,d\theta=\frac16(\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta)$. Check: differentiating gives the integrand. Answer: $\frac{t \sqrt{1 - 9 t^{2}}}{2} + \frac{\arcsin{\left(3 t \right)}}{6}+C$.
Worked example 4 · Partial fractions (Computation problems 10, Q4)
Evaluate $\displaystyle\int \dfrac{x^2+4x+1}{(x-1)(x+1)(x+3)}\,dx$.
Show the answer and the solution

Answer: $\frac{3 \ln{\left(x - 1 \right)}}{4} + \frac{\ln{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{2} - \frac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4}+C$

Cover-up: $A=\frac{1+4+1}{2\cdot4}=\frac34$, $B=\frac{1-4+1}{(-2)(2)}=\frac12$, $C=\frac{9-12+1}{(-4)(-2)}=-\frac14$. Check: differentiating gives the integrand. Answer: $\frac{3 \ln{\left(x - 1 \right)}}{4} + \frac{\ln{\left(x + 1 \right)}}{2} - \frac{\ln{\left(x + 3 \right)}}{4}+C$.
