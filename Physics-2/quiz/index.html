University of Science and Technology of Hanoi · B1 · Fundamental Physics II (PHY1.002)

Unofficial self-study material prepared from the USTH Fundamental Physics II course documents (not endorsed by USTH). Problem statements are paraphrased with their source cited on every question; solutions, explanations and checks are our own. Every numerical answer is computed by program from the physical law and re-derived by a second, independent route (another formula, energy instead of kinematics, numerical integration or simulation, ray-transfer matrices); differences from the printed answers of the course sheets are listed on the Sources page. Progress is stored only in your browser.

Source documents used
  • Lectures — Electricity, Magnetism and Optics lecture slides (AY 2024–25 and 2025–26)
  • Tutorials — Homework sessions 1–3 (Halliday problems) and tutorial chapters 33–36 (Young and Freedman problems)
  • Tutoring slides — Midterm and final tutorials and formula sheets by the Learning Support team (scanned slides, OCR read)
  • Textbooks — Halliday, Resnick and Walker, Fundamentals of Physics and Young and Freedman, University Physics with Modern Physics
