How to train with this site

  1. Learn the chapter from your lecture slides and the textbook sections listed in the Sources page.
  2. Quiz it: do the three sets of that chapter. Read every explanation, also for the questions you got right. Use "Redo only the mistakes".
  3. Go deeper in the practice bank: tick written mode to hide the options, solve on paper, then compare with the model answer.
  4. Simulate the exams: the mock midterm (no going back), the 40-question mock final, then the 30-question / 20-minute speed exam. Review every explanation afterwards, also for the questions you got right.
  5. Repeat the weak chapters shown in the progress panel above until each is above 75%.

About the course (Course advice sheet by Bui Thien Truong, Hoang Tien Anh and Huynh Nguyen Van Anh (AY 2025–26))

ComponentWeightDetails
Attendance10%lecture and tutor class
Midterm30%MCQ test on Moodle (the 2025–26 midterm tutoring covers chapters 1–6: Coulomb's law up to the magnetic fields of currents)
Final60%MCQ test on Moodle (number of questions and duration are not stated in the documents; the mocks here use 40 questions and 80 minutes, our assumption)
  • Three parts: Electricity (Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss' law, electric potential), Magnetism (magnetic force, magnetic field, induction) and Optics (nature and propagation of light, geometric optics, interference, diffraction).
  • One of the harder B1 courses because of the sheer number of formulae in every part. Electricity is easy if you follow the lectures; Magnetism needs more mathematics; Optics is the easiest but is stuffed with formulae.
  • Bonus points: up to 6 points can be added to the midterm (any excess goes to the final).
  • Visualise: pictures and videos help a lot with fields, forces and rays.
