University of Science and Technology of Hanoi · B1 · Fundamental Physics I (PHY1.001)

Unofficial self-study material prepared from the USTH Fundamental Physics I course documents (not endorsed by USTH). Problem statements are paraphrased with their source cited on every question; solutions, explanations and checks are our own. Every numerical answer is computed by program from the physical law and re-derived by a second, independent route (another formula, energy instead of kinematics, numerical integration or simulation, ray-transfer matrices); differences from the printed answers of the course sheets are listed on the Sources page. Progress is stored only in your browser.

Source documents used
  • Lectures — Mechanics lectures 1–5 (AY 2024–25 and 2026–27) and Thermodynamics parts I–III
  • Tutorials — Mechanics tutorial Parts 1–5 and Thermodynamics tutorial Parts I, II (1), II (2), III
  • Tutoring slides — Midterm and final tutorials by the Learning Support team (scanned slides, OCR read)
  • Textbooks — Halliday, Resnick and Walker, Fundamentals of Physics (main book) and Young and Freedman, University Physics
